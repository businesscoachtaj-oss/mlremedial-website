What I do

Remedial massage, built around your training

Every session is tailored. Whether you are rehabbing an old injury, chasing a personal best or just tired of feeling stiff, we start with what is holding you back and work on that first.

  • A quick check-in at the start of every session
  • Hands-on work on what is tight or sore that day
  • Simple stretches or cues to keep progress going at home
  • Weekly, fortnightly or monthly plans to suit how hard you train
  • Online training is also available on enquiry
