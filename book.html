Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay before my session?

Yes. All sessions are booked and paid online through your plan before your appointment. There is no option to pay on the day.

Can I claim it on my health fund?

May is a registered member of the Association of Massage Therapists, so many clients can claim a remedial massage rebate through private health insurance. Check your own cover with your fund.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing that gives easy access to the areas being worked on. Activewear works well.

What is your cancellation policy?

Plans are billed automatically through Stripe and can be cancelled any time. To move or cancel an appointment, message May on Instagram or call 0439 320 399 as early as you can.
