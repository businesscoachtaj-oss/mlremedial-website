My Approach

From the gym floor to helping others move better

Being active as a child and playing a lot of sport, I dedicated most of my time to training into my late teens — Muay Thai kickboxing, which then carried over into weight training. Being able to perform in sport has always kept me wanting to perform at my best, and because of that I became part of the furniture in the gym — and felt some pain along the way. My history with back pain has always kept me humble and curious to learn more about rehab and prehab training, and to apply it to people going through similar things.

I've been in the health and fitness industry for well over 10 years as a coach, and using stretch therapy for 4 of those. I've been fortunate to learn from coaches in the industry who are in it for better health, and to be mentored by people who really thrive with that knowledge — an approach to my own training and to helping others that's had a real impact on the people I work with.

My mission is to enhance overall wellbeing by providing personalised, hands-on therapy that promotes flexibility, mobility and pain relief — helping people of all ages and activity levels improve their physical health, prevent injury and reduce stress.
