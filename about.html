My story

From the gym floor to helping others move better

Being active as a child and playing a lot of sport, I dedicated most of my time to training into my late teens. Muay Thai kickboxing came first, and that carried over into weight training. Wanting to perform at my best kept me in the gym so much I became part of the furniture, and I felt some pain along the way.

My own history with back pain keeps me humble and curious about rehab and prehab, and about applying it to people going through the same thing. I have been in the health and fitness industry for well over 10 years as a coach, using stretch therapy for 4 of those, and I have been lucky to learn from coaches and mentors who are in it for better health.

My mission is to improve overall wellbeing with personalised, hands-on therapy that promotes flexibility, mobility and pain relief, helping people of all ages and activity levels improve their physical health, prevent injury and reduce stress.
